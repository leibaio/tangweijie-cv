项目背景Context

平台服务多国作者，既有 C 端官网与阅读器，也有 B 端创作、合同、税务和结算后台。前端需要同时处理复杂的表单流程、金额数据、PDF 生成和跨设备体验。 The platform serves authors across countries with both a public site and a B2B backend for creation, contracts, tax and settlement. The frontend handles complex forms, financial data, PDF generation and cross-device experience.

关键设计Key decisions

  • 敏感税务数据在浏览器端完成 PDF 生成与签名，不经过服务端。Sensitive tax data is rendered and signed entirely in the browser, never sent through the server.
  • 先用 performance.now() 分段埋点定位渲染瓶颈，再做收窄渲染区域、复用上下文、拆分长任务。Located the rendering bottleneck with performance.now() instrumentation first, then narrowed the render area, reused the context and split long tasks.
  • 多国税表用模板 + 字段配置驱动，避免为每个国家复制一套页面。Multi-country tax forms are driven by templates and field config, avoiding a separate page per country.

我做了什么What I built

  • 三种投稿路径：CFL / WFH / Ongoing，分步表单、预览与状态流转，覆盖从创建到提交审核的完整链路。Three submission paths — CFL, WFH and ongoing — with step forms, previews and status transitions from creation to review.
  • 封面生成与裁剪：html2canvas + cropperjs；分段埋点定位瓶颈后，单次生成 3000ms → 500ms。Cover generation and cropping with html2canvas + cropperjs; after instrumentation, generation dropped from 3000ms to 500ms.
  • 税表 PDF 与电子签：pdf-lib 加载模板 + drawText 填充；signature_pad 手写签名经 toDataURL → embedPng → drawImage 叠加到指定坐标，支持多国模板。Tax-form PDFs and e-signature: pdf-lib templates + drawText; signature_pad handwriting goes toDataURL → embedPng → drawImage at precise coordinates, with multi-country templates.
  • 收入与合同中心：ECharts 收入趋势、结算明细、合同 / 税表状态、Payoneer / PayPal / 银行账户配置。Income and contract center: ECharts revenue trends, settlement details, contract / tax status and Payoneer / PayPal / bank account setup.
  • 性能与体验：p-queue 批量上传并发限制 10、图片懒加载、Lottie 升级动画、PC / 移动响应式。Performance and UX: p-queue batch uploads capped at 10, image lazy loading, Lottie level-up animations and responsive PC / mobile layouts.
